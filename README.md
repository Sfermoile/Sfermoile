About Me:

Hello! My name is Shawn Fermoile, a passionate and dedicated cybersecurity enthusiast with a focus on blue team operations and a long-term goal of penetration testing, red teaming, and ethical hacking. My journey in the world of cybersecurity began as a curious kid exploring BackTrack Linux, and it has led me to explore various tools, techniques, and methodologies essential for identifying, detecting, and mitigating security vulnerabilities. 

I hold an A.A.S. in Information Assurance and Cybersecurity and the Google Cybersecurity Certificate. I am currently working towards my CompTIA Security+ as well as the HTB Certified Defensive Security Analyst (CDSA). I continually refine my skills through hands-on challenges and real-world scenarios in my own home lab and on Hack The Box.

I am also building **Mindful Metrics**, an app that tracks both mental and physical health statistics and correlates the two with personal actions, helping users make better choices, feel happier and more fulfilled, and reach their goals. Because health data is some of the most sensitive data there is, I'm designing it to be local-first: users' data stays on their own device and is never sent to or stored on my servers, so they are in full control of it. 

Before cybersecurity, I spent years managing high-volume food and beverage operations at resort properties, which taught me to stay calm under pressure, prioritize, and communicate clearly with teams, all while being able to de-escalate situations as needed.

I am currently looking for a SOC analyst or desktop support role where I can keep growing in the cybersecurity and tech fields.
